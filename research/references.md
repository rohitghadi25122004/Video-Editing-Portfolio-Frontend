Patterns only. All URLs returned HTTP 200 on 2026-10-01.

## Sam Kolder (filmmaker, editor)
https://www.samkolder.com
- Silent Teaser, Sound on Demand: muted, looping, transcoded MP4/WebM showreel (with poster frame) fills the hero; a play button opens the full reel with sound in a lightbox (Morphing Modal territory).
- Contact: mailto with a prefilled subject, repeated in a closing CTA section and footer socials.

## Ordinary Folk (motion design studio, Jorge Canedo)
https://ordinaryfolk.co
- Bento Grid of work cards in mixed sizes (large, medium, wide), each a muted autoplay loop, so the grid itself is the reel.
- Hover-Play Cards plus a lightbox reel; Magnetic Button on the reel play control.
- Contact is a plain mailto (prefilled subject) and phone, no form.

## Matheus M. Bianchi (motion designer)
https://matheus.works
- Reel leads, embedded via youtube-nocookie with autoplay off and controls on.
- Timecoded Credit List: reel timestamps listed with his role on each shot (design, animation, or both). This is a motivated editor motif: it answers "what did you actually do?".
- Contact: short list of email, Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance.

## Sahil Lulla (commercial and trailer editor)
https://sahillulla.webflow.io
- Muted background video loop behind an Asymmetric Split Hero (headshot plus tagline).
- Projects in a carousel, opened in a lightbox; 9:16 and 16:9 pieces share identical square-ish thumbnails, so vertical work loses its shape.
- Contact: email, phone, short form.

## Tim Beeston (documentary editor)
https://www.tim-beeston.com
- Credits-style thumbnail grid; every tile links out to IMDB, YouTube or streaming sites.

## Anti-patterns to avoid
- Link-outs instead of in-page playback (Beeston): the visitor leaves the site at the moment of highest interest.
- Forcing vertical work into the same landscape or square thumbnail as horizontal (Lulla): crops faces and captions, hides the Reels skill.
- Client logo strip and long tagline stacked inside the hero (Lulla, Kolder).

Note: none of the five sites handled 9:16 and 16:9 together well. That is the gap Vasant can own.

## Takeaways for Vasant
- Hero: one muted, looping, poster-backed cut of the 16:9 long-form piece (Silent Teaser) plus one "watch with sound" control opening a modal. No logo strip in the hero.
- Verticals: present the three 9:16 pieces at their native ratio, side by side as a row of three phone-proportioned cards (horizontal scroll-snap on mobile), never cropped to squares.
- Players: Hover-Play Cards on desktop (muted preview), tap-to-play on touch; lazy facade (poster image) until interaction, youtube-nocookie or self-hosted MP4 with preload="none".
- One motivated editor motif only: a Timecoded Credit List under each video (hook, captions, motion graphics, color, with timestamps that seek the player). Skip decorative timelines and fake scrubbers.
- Contact: email as the primary action with a copy-to-clipboard button plus mailto, Instagram second; repeat at page end. No form needed.
- Attach one honest outcome per piece (views, retention, turnaround) where Vasant has real numbers.
